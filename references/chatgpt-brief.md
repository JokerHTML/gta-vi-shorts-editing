# ChatGPT Brief (Step 1)

Step 1 tells you to paste a brief into a NEW ChatGPT conversation. This
file IS that brief: the first half is the original brief used for the
Nov 15 trio, copied verbatim. The second half is a fill-in template for
a new topic, built from the fixed brief elements that are identical
across all three Nov 15 briefs.

FACTS (use only these — do not add, infer, or invent anything):
1. GTA VI launches Thursday, November nineteenth, on PlayStation Five and Xbox Series X and S.
2. On Friday, November thirteenth, DiscussingFilm exclusively debuted a teaser of about thirty seconds for Macca & Friends, an animated series that plays inside GTA VI. Rockstar reshared the teaser on its own accounts a few hours later. Rockstar posted no standalone Newswire article about it.
3. The official blurb, in full: "The series follows the adventures of Macca the Gator and his wetland friends across the state of Leonida. Coming November 19 on WEAZEL."
4. Macca is a pink alligator. The teaser shows him owing rent to his crab landlord and swinging a baseball bat; the crew includes a manatee, a pink flamingo and a talking sun; scenes include an airboat ride and a lowrider cruise.
5. Do NOT name the crab landlord — outlets disagree (Pinche vs Pinchy) and Rockstar's own blurb names no one. Say "his crab landlord" only.
6. WEAZEL is the fictional TV network inside GTA. The series premieres on WEAZEL on November nineteenth, the same day the game launches.
7. Macca was already the face of the three-hundred-ninety-nine-dollar Collector's Box and the Goodtime State merch collection.
8. Press and fans compared the teaser's look to Adult Swim shows. Say "fans compared it to" — never as fact.
9. Unconfirmed: episode count, episode length, and whether full episodes are watchable start to finish. Say that plainly — "nobody outside Rockstar has said" — never claim it.
10. Four days remain from today (Sunday, November fifteenth) to the November nineteenth launch.

- Do NOT name the crab landlord. Do NOT say "Rockstar announced" about the show. Do NOT say "yesterday" for November thirteenth (that was Friday). Do NOT say "tomorrow" for the launch. No leaked material, no "leak" word, no product link.

End with "Sources: DiscussingFilm exclusive (Nov 13, 2026); Gematsu (Nov 13, 2026); Beebom (Nov 13, 2026); Rockstar Games reshare." Then the exact line "Four days to launch." No product links.

## After ChatGPT returns

Run the Step 2 honesty check before any build: hand-recount the script
to 90-105 words (tokens and spoken words, both inside the gate; see
the word-count convention in SKILL.md), check every fact traces to a
ledger row, check the banned list is clean, check attribution is varied
(M18, M26), check the countdown is exact for the ACTUAL air date (M30),
check the title character count is verbatim, check the description
Sources line is present, check the 12 tags are in order, check the CATEGORY
line is present with its one-line reasoning, check any negative claim names
its seller/distributor check (M32), check there
is no product link, and check the thumbnail brief cover text is verbatim
with base and backup frames and the safe-margin note present. A return
that fails any of these goes back to ChatGPT in the SAME conversation
with the failing line quoted; never fix a ChatGPT line by hand.
